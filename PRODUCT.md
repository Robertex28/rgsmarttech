# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Individuals and small businesses in Barquisimeto, Cabudare, and the surrounding Zona Industrial area who need reliable tech help: PC/laptop repair, custom builds, home/office networking, or CCTV installation. They are non-technical — they know something is broken or needed, not how to fix it. Their primary concern is trust: "Is this person real? Can I count on them?" They reach out via WhatsApp or a contact form; the decision happens on mobile.

## Product Purpose

Convert site visitors into clients. The landing page is the entire front door of the business. Success means a potential client feeling confident enough to tap the WhatsApp button. Secondary purpose: SEO visibility so people searching "técnico PC Barquisimeto" land here rather than a competitor.

## Brand Personality

Confiable, experto, preciso. The feeling to produce: the professional you are relieved to have found. Not a corporate call center. Not a friendly hobbyist. Someone who has seen this problem a hundred times and will handle it right.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS / tech startup (dark mode + cyan-blue gradient): the current design falls into this category. That aesthetic reads as "template-built startup," not a local expert with 20 years on the ground.
- Old-school local repair shop: cluttered, cheap-looking, price-list energy. Undermines the expert positioning.
- Corporate IT vendor (IBM / Cisco style): navy-formal, impersonal, built for enterprise. Wrong scale, wrong register.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence over claims — demonstrate 20 years of expertise through specificity and quiet confidence, not superlatives or generic badge icons.
2. Trust before transaction — the primary job is earning confidence. Every visual decision should make the client feel safer, not more impressed.
3. Local and precise — the site is for Barquisimeto. Real person, real place, real guarantee. That specificity is a differentiator; do not dilute it into generic tech aesthetics.
4. Earned restraint — an expert does not need to shout. Quiet, confident design outperforms flashy effects for this brand.
5. Mobile-first contact path — the WhatsApp CTA is the most important element. Every layout decision should serve that conversion.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Spanish-only content. No known user-specific requirements beyond standard compliance: good contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader basics.
